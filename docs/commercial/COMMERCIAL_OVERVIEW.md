# Commercial and pilot blueprint (source-derived, not validated)

**Source:** supplied SiteGuard QR blueprint §7, pages 6–7. **Scope:** business proposal/context, not software implementation or legal/commercial advice. Listed prices, ROI assumptions and market statistics are unverified and may change.

## 1. Positioning

The blueprint positions SiteGuard QR as a **Labor Cost Control & Anti-Fraud Audit System**, charged by active scanned workers, for construction firms and public infrastructure/government engineering offices. It targets ghost-worker risk, buddy punching, unapproved overtime, fingerprint-scan issues and poor audit trails. Claims about 5–12% leakage and 30%+ fingerprint failure are **source claims without cited studies**; validate before external publication.

## 3. Source ROI example

Blueprint assumptions: 180 workers across 3 sites, $14/hour blended, stated gross field payroll $276,480/month, leakage recovery 7.5%, monthly recovered labor $20,736, annual $248,832. Contractor Pro: $249 + (180 × $1.25) = **$474/month**, **$5,688/year**. Annual net as source: $248,832 − $5,688 = **$243,144/year**.

Blueprint also states **eight-day payback**; method, deployment cost assumptions and calculation are not provided, so this is **not independently substantiated**. Do not imply expected customer savings or a verified eight-day return absent a defensible baseline and sensitivity analysis.

## 4. 90-day sales narrative from source

- **Days 1–30:** “15-day zero-risk shadow pilot.” One gate kiosk for a construction firm with 80–250 workers; deploy days 1–2; compare with existing attendance from days 3–14; **do not alter current payouts**.
- **Days 31–45:** quantify discrepancies and present side-by-side day-15 variance evidence; seek paid annual Contractor Pro conversion.
- **Days 46–90:** expand regionally and approach engineering/public works offices with documented cases, auditability and reviewed Form 48 output.

## 5. Pilot measurement plan (proposed)

Before measuring: obtain privacy/employee notice approvals, define treatment of disputed punches and a benchmark ground truth; do not equate a missing scan with fraud. Suggested metrics: valid scan completion rate, unverified exception rate, delayed offline receipts, verified vs rejected overtime, reviewer SLA, false-positive rate, supervisor time saved and measured payroll variance after independent reconciliation.

A pilot should be **shadow-only** until policy, risk controls, identity management, audit invariants and employee-data protections pass release gates. Real wage decisions must stay in the existing authorized payroll process until signed off.
